## 0. READ FIRST: truth rules for this account

1. **No income or client-count claims.** Nothing in this calendar says "$110k MRR", "I have X clients" or "my clients get X leads". Posts that mention numbers are labelled hypothetical ("the math", "if you…") or come from the book's own made-up-for-illustration tables.
2. **Stories are the book's stories** (forklift years, the TikTok-mom rabbit hole, the first Loom client, the truck listing, Aaron, the plumber under the sink). Don't add invented ones. Where a real number, date or result would make a post stronger, it's marked `[FILL: …]`. If you don't have it, delete the line.
3. **Bio:** drop "$110k MRR". Pick one:
   - `faceless. building a GHL smart-website agency for local service businesses. posting the playbook.`
   - `GHL website reseller. sharing the exact steps to land local service clients for $0.`
   - `introvert. ex-warehouse. smart websites for local trades. the 30-step playbook, free.`
4. **Authority posts critique ideas and models, not people.** The book credits Kai and Pavel for popularising the model, so the posts do too: "great product model, missing the get-clients part".
5. **Pinned post:** Day 1, Post 1 (the 30-step overview). Update the pin after week 3 with the full list (Day 21, Post 1).

## Day 4 (Thu)

**Post 1 · [E] · step 7**
```
step 7 of 30: learn the one-job rule

when an owner looks at $297 a month, they're not thinking about $297

they're thinking: "will this pay for itself?"

answer before they ask. take one average job in their trade and divide it by $297

- $300 regular clean = 1 month
- $900 plumbing callout = 3 months
- $2,500 fence = 8 months
- $8,000 re-roof = 2 years+

(rough examples. ask the owner what a job is worth. they'll tell you)

you're not asking a roofer to spend $297 a month

you're asking whether one extra roof in the next two years sounds possible

that's a very different conversation
```

**Post 2 · [E] · step 8**
```
step 8 of 30: answer the four silent questions

every owner you pitch is asking these. they'll almost never say them out loud

1. what does it cost me today?
2. how long until it's working?
3. how much of my time is this going to eat?
4. what if it doesn't work?

how the typical agency answers: $3-8k. 6-12 weeks. three calls and two rounds of feedback. well, you've already paid

how yours answers: $297, no setup fee. live in 7 days. a 20-minute form. cancel anytime

four easy answers

you're not convincing anyone of anything. you're making sure they hear them
```

```
step 17 of 30: hold the stop/slow bat, and follow up once

i did traffic control for a while. the person holding the sign controls the whole road. nobody moves until they flip the bat

in sales, whoever holds the bat controls what happens next. most people hand it to the prospect:
- "let me know what you think"
- "get back to me whenever"
```
