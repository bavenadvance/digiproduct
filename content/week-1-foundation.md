in 1956 a guy at a small swedish furniture company couldn't fit a table in his car. so he took the legs off

- learn: how a GoHighLevel website, its forms and its workflows fit together

- learn: same offer, same price, same answers to the same five questions
- practice: write your pitch once and read it aloud until it sounds like you

- why: one-off builds make you start from zero every month. recurring stacks
